INDUSTRY CASE STUDY 1 · USABILITY FAILURE

Humane AI Pin: Why a $699 Gadget Failed

Engineering Vision vs Real-World Usability
  • Engineering Vision: Screenless $699 pin aiming to replace smartphones using voice & laser palm projection.
  • Why It Failed: Laser display was unreadable in sunlight; high voice latency; severe overheating.
  • Design Thinking Flaw: Misread user behavior — users didn't want to replace their smartphones!
Key Takeaway
A futuristic product with impressive engineering will fail if it ignores real human constraints and usability.

INDUSTRY CASE STUDY 2 · EMBRACING EMPATHY

Airbnb 2009: The Photography Turnaround

Joe Gebbia & Brian Chesky Case Study
  • Friction Point: In 2009, Airbnb revenue flatlined at $200/week. Listings were getting zero bookings.
  • Empathy Discovery: Founders flew to NYC and looked at site photos. Photos were blurry, dark cell phone shots!
  • Non-Scalable Solution: They bought a camera, visited NYC hosts, and took professional photos manually.
  • Result: Weekly revenue doubled to $400 in 7 days, launching Airbnb to a $100B valuation!
Design Lesson
"Do things that don't scale first!" Direct human empathy reveals insights data analytics can never show.

INDUSTRY CASE STUDY 3 · EXTREME USER EMPATHY

Nike FlyEase: Designing for Extreme Users

Matthew Walzer & Tobie Hatfield (Nike)
  • Friction Point: 16-year-old Matthew Walzer (Cerebral Palsy) wrote to Nike asking for shoes he could put on independently without assistance.
  • Extreme User Insight: Designing for a user with physical limitations created a hands-free zipper/hinge entry mechanism.
  • Mass Adoption: FlyEase became a global bestseller adopted by athletes, elderly users, and busy parents everywhere!
Design Principle
"Solve for the extreme, and you benefit the mainstream!" Extreme user empathy creates universally accessible products.

INDUSTRY CASE STUDY 4 · FRUGAL INNOVATION

Embrace Infant Warmer: The $25 Life-Saver

Stanford d.school Extreme Affordable Design
  • Friction Point: 4 million premature infants die annually in developing rural areas because traditional hospital incubators cost $20,000+ and require continuous electricity.
  • Frugal Solution: Designed a $25 sleeping bag containing phase-change wax heated by boiling water, keeping babies warm for 6+ hours without electricity!
  • Impact: Saved over 300,000 premature babies across rural India and 20+ countries!
Stanford Case Study
Frugal Innovation isn't about cheap materials — it's about radical empathy and removing unnecessary complexity!

INDUSTRY CASE STUDY 5 · CROWDSTRIKE 2024 OUTAGE

July 2024 Global Outage: 5-Why Root Cause Analysis

5-Why Incident Analysis (8.5M Windows Crashes)
  • 1. Why BSOD? Memory read out-of-bounds error in Falcon Sensor driver.
  • 2. Why Memory Error? Channel File 291 content update had 21 fields vs 20 expected fields.
  • 3. Why Uncaught? Missing runtime array bounds check in Content Interpreter.
  • 4. Why Deployed? Staged canary deployment was skipped; pushed to all machines globally!
  • ROOT CAUSE: System design prioritized rapid update delivery over fault-tolerant staged rollouts.
Engineering Takeaway
High-impact software systems must fail gracefully! Staged rollouts and input validation prevent global catastrophes.

CORE VISUAL · THE 5 STAGES

The 5 Iterative Stages Flowchart

Iterative Innovation Loop
  • 1. Empathize: Understand human needs & struggles.
  • 2. Define: Frame a focused problem statement.
  • 3. Ideate: Brainstorm wide creative solutions.
  • 4. Prototype: Build quick, low-cost mockups.
  • 5. Test: Gather user feedback & iterate.
CASE STUDY · GE PEDIATRIC MRI

Doug Dietz Empathy Transformation

Pirate Ship Adventure Series
  • Friction: 80% of pediatric patients required heavy sedation due to terror inside dark MRI machines.
  • Empathy Insight: Children don't fear medical technology; they fear the dark, scary machine.
  • Result: Redesigned as Pirate Ship → Sedation rates dropped under 1%!
CASE STUDY · INDIAN TEA STALL UPI

Paytm / PhonePe UPI Soundbox

Micro-Merchant Empathy
  • Friction: Cash change shortage (`"No cash change"`) for ₹10 tea causing customer drop-offs.
  • Empathy Insight: Vendor needed instant payment voice alerts without stopping tea brewing or touching phone screen with wet hands.
  • Result: Audio Soundbox speaker digitized millions of Indian street vendors!
